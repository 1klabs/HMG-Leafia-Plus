NEMESIS deep collection (read-only) - for porting wireless CarPlay/AA

1. Copy NEMESIS.data and NEMESIS.sh to the root of an exFAT/FAT32 USB drive
   with at least 200 MB free. No other NEMESIS files on the drive.
2. Optional but helpful: just before, try connecting the iPhone wirelessly
   so the projection log has something in it.
3. Car on, plug in the drive, tap Yes. Wait for the success message (1-2 min).
4. Zip the NEMESIS-deep folder and send it privately.
   NEMESIS-deep/PRIVATE holds firmware key files: never post those publicly.

Nothing on the car is changed. Nothing from your accounts, phones, Wi-Fi
passwords, the vehicle security partition, VIN or serial is collected.
